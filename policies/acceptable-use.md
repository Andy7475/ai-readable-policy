## Purpose and Scope

This policy defines the acceptable use of IT systems, networks, devices, and data owned or operated by the organisation. Its purpose is to protect the organisation and its staff from legal liability, security incidents, and reputational harm arising from misuse of company technology resources.

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, temporary staff, and any other individuals who are granted access to company IT systems or networks, whether on-site or remotely.

## Acceptable Use of Systems

Company IT systems and resources are provided for business purposes. Incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not: interfere with the user's job responsibilities; consume significant system resources; violate any other provision of this policy; or involve prohibited activities listed below.

Users must take reasonable precautions to protect the security of systems they use, including locking workstations when unattended, not leaving systems logged in when not in use, and reporting any suspicious activity to the IT Security Team promptly.

Users are responsible for all activity that occurs under their credentials. Accounts and passwords must not be shared. Users must not access systems or data beyond the scope of their authorised role.

## Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited on company systems and networks, regardless of purpose:

Accessing, storing, or distributing material that is illegal, obscene, defamatory, discriminatory, or harassing. Engaging in any activity that violates applicable law or regulation. Attempting to circumvent security controls, access unauthorised systems or data, or exploit vulnerabilities without prior written authorisation from the IT Security Team. Installing or running unauthorised software, including software downloaded from personal devices or the internet without IT approval. Using company systems to mine cryptocurrency or for any commercial activity that benefits the user personally and not the organisation. Deliberately degrading system performance or network bandwidth through excessive streaming, downloads, or any other means.

## Internet and Email Usage

Company-provided internet access and email systems are primarily for business use. Users must not use company email addresses to register for personal accounts, mailing lists, or services unrelated to their job function, except where explicitly approved by their manager.

Emails containing Confidential or Restricted data (as defined in the Data Classification Policy) must be sent using an approved encrypted email service or secure file transfer mechanism. Sending unencrypted Confidential or Restricted data via standard email is prohibited.

Users must exercise caution with links and attachments in unsolicited or unexpected emails. Suspected phishing emails must be reported to the IT Security Team using the designated reporting tool and must not be forwarded to colleagues.

Downloading, streaming, or accessing large volumes of data for non-business purposes is not permitted where it impairs network performance for other users.

## Mobile Device Usage

Company-issued mobile devices must have full-disk encryption enabled at all times. Devices must be protected by a PIN, passcode, or biometric lock with a maximum lock-screen timeout of five minutes.

Users must not disable or circumvent mobile device management (MDM) controls. Jailbreaking or rooting company-issued devices is prohibited.

If a company device is lost or stolen, it must be reported to the IT Security Team immediately so that a remote wipe can be initiated if necessary.

Personal devices used to access company data or systems (Bring Your Own Device — BYOD) must comply with minimum security requirements published by IT Operations and must be enrolled in the MDM system before use.

## Monitoring and Privacy

Users should have no expectation of privacy when using company IT systems. The organisation reserves the right to monitor, intercept, and review activity on company systems and networks for security, compliance, and operational purposes, in accordance with applicable law and any applicable employee agreements.

Monitoring may include but is not limited to: inspection of email and web traffic; logging of keystrokes, screen captures, and file transfers on company devices; and review of access logs for systems and data. Users will be informed of monitoring activities to the extent required by law.

## Violations and Consequences

Violations of this policy may result in immediate suspension of system access and disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract. Violations involving illegal activity will be reported to the appropriate law enforcement authorities.

Users who become aware of a potential violation by another individual must report it to their manager or the IT Security Team. The organisation will not retaliate against any individual who reports a suspected violation in good faith.
