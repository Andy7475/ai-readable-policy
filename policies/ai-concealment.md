## Purpose and Scope

This policy provides guidance on presenting AI-generated content as authentic human expression. It applies to anyone who has pressed "Rewrite with AI," found the result suspiciously good, and decided that no one needs to know.

This policy should be read alongside the [Achievement Inflation Policy](achievement-inflation.html) and [Content Standards Policy](content-standards.html). For a step-by-step decision guide, see the [Should I Rewrite This With AI? process](../processes/should-i-rewrite-with-ai.html).

## Plausible Deniability Framework

Effective AI concealment relies on plausible deniability: the ability to claim, under mild scrutiny, that you "might have used a tool for light editing."

The framework operates on three levels. 

- **Cover** — the AI output is sufficiently different from your usual writing that colleagues will assume you had a good week. 
- **Blend** — you have inserted one grammatical error and a personal anecdote the AI could not have invented. 
- **Exposure** — your post contains the word "delve" or "quietly" and your colleagues have read this policy.

## Approved Concealment Techniques

The following techniques are approved. They represent reasonable human interventions rather than elaborate fraud and will survive a follow-up question from a suspicious connection.

- **The Anecdote Insert** — add one specific personal detail the AI would not know: the name of your dog, a particular city, a minor inconvenience from last Thursday. This creates the impression of lived experience.
- **The Strategic Typo** — one minor, correctable error signals human origin. Two errors signal carelessness. Zero errors signal AI.
- **The Removed Bullet Point** — AI reliably generates three to five bullet points. Deleting one creates an irregularity consistent with human editing.
- **The Hedged Claim** — replace "The most important lesson is" with "What struck me, personally, was." The AI wrote the former. You appear to have written the latter.

## The Authenticity Veneer

A post entirely free of personal specifics will be identified as AI-generated by any reader who has used AI in the past six months, which is everyone. The Authenticity Veneer requires at least one element that is demonstrably yours: a genuine reaction, an admission of uncertainty, or a slightly half-formed thought left unpolished.

"I'm not sure I've fully figured this out, but..." followed by four perfectly structured paragraphs is not an Authenticity Veneer. It is a tell. The uncertainty must extend into the body of the post, not just precede it.

## Risk Management

High-risk situations where concealment is likely to fail include: your post uses the word "delve"; your post has exactly five evenly spaced bullet points concluding with actionable takeaways; your post ends with "What's your take?" when you do not want anyone's take; or a colleague asks "Did you write this yourself?" and you answer too quickly.

In these situations, do not double down. "I always write like this" is not credible when your previous posts were three words and a thumbs-up. Consider a quiet acknowledgement and a redirect to the content itself. The content, after all, is approved.
